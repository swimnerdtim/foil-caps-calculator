Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a dome cap and a regular swim cap?

A dome swim cap is molded into a smooth, rounded shape so it fits wrinkle-free and reduces drag — which is why it's the go-to racing cap for competitive swimmers. Standard flat caps tend to wrinkle, creating drag that can slow you down.

Are these good competition swim caps?

Yes. Both our dome and spiral finishes are premium silicone racing caps designed for competition, offering a streamlined, hydrodynamic fit for meets and time trials.

Can I put my team logo and swimmer names on the caps?

Absolutely. Choose Team Design for your logo and colors (up to 4), or Fully Personalized to add each swimmer's name to their own cap.
